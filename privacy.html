content-bank privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026.

content-bank is used only by its owner. It asks for access to the owner's Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive) so that rclone can upload, list, download and delete the owner's own files.

This data is used only for the owner's own file storage and processing. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone except the processing services the owner chooses for their own work.

Access tokens are kept only on the owner's computer and in the owner's private notebook settings. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
